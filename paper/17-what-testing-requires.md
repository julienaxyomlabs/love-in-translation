## Designs and what each can establish

Eight designs identify different parts of the model:
1. **Cross-sectional dyadic surveys** with commensurate need and supply, analysed with dyadic response surface analysis, separate level from mismatch associationally.
2. **Dyadic daily diaries** of four weeks or more identify detection, truth and bias for each link, multilevel response surfaces and within-dyad dynamics ([Laurenceau & Bolger 2005](https://doi.org/10.1037/0893-3200.19.1.86)). Three to four weeks is a practical minimum. It is enough to estimate average autoregressive and coupling parameters across many dyads but weak for any single dyad, and short series bias state-noise estimates ([Song & Ferrer 2009](https://doi.org/10.1080/10705510902751432)). The number of days and dyads should be set by simulation ([Schultzberg & Muthén 2018](https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2017.1392862); [Lafit et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1177/2515245920978738)) [Moderate/mixed, methodological judgment].
3. **Laboratory interactions with segment-by-segment ratings and observer coding** test accuracy against a third-party criterion ([Overall, Fletcher & Kenny 2012](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167211432764)).
4. **Factorial experiments with standardized messages**, crossing amount with channel match, give expressed care a true ground truth ([Collins & Feeney 2004](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.87.3.363)).
5. **Within-couple or within-family micro-randomized trials** identify the causal "excursion effects" of channel match and amount ([Klasnja et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1037/hea0000305); [Qian et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1037/met0000283)).
6. **Family round-robin designs** identify relationship-specific misperception ([Cook 1994](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-006X.62.3.500)).
7. **Long panels** describe trajectories, with attention to selection artefacts ([Bühler & Orth 2025](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000551)).
8. **Cross-cultural invariance studies with calibration vignettes** test whether comparisons across groups are valid.

**Correlation, prediction and causation must be kept apart:**
- Designs 1–3 and 6–7 yield associations.
- Machine-learning benchmarks yield prediction ([Joel et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1917036117)).
- Only designs 4–5 identify causal effects, and only of the manipulated components.

Within-person data are helpful but neither necessary nor sufficient for causal inference, because time-varying confounders produce spurious within-person associations ([Rohrer & Murayama 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/25152459221140842)). The classic cross-lagged panel model is "almost never the right choice" ([Lucas 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/25152459231158378)). The random-intercept version depends on parametric assumptions ([Lüdtke & Robitzsch 2022](https://doi.org/10.1080/10705511.2022.2065278)) [Established]. Every causal claim should be preceded by a causal graph of its assumptions ([Rohrer 2018](https://doi.org/10.1177/2515245917745629)).
